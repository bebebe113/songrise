Songrise Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

The App collects the following information:
・The identifier needed to sign in with your Apple Account (we don't collect your email address). The name Apple provides the first time is used as your initial display name
・Your registered ID, display name, and profile photo
・The song you're listening to and songs you recently played (you can choose "Don't Show" in Sharing)
・Songs and notes you post, likes, library saves, friendships, blocks, and reports
・The last time you opened the App (to show friends you're online), your time zone, and the App's display language (to send notifications in that language)

How We Use Information

We use this information to show your songs to friends, build playlists, send notifications, prevent abuse, and respond to inquiries.

Who Can See Your Information

Your songs, posts, and profile are shown only to people you've accepted as friends. However, songs you post (title, artist, and artwork) appear for 24 hours, without identifying you, in a playlist all users can see. Your name and notes aren't shown there. If you allow ID search, anyone who types your full ID can see your display name and the song you're listening to (you can turn this off in Settings).

Ads

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. Only with your permission, your device's advertising identifier is shared with Google to personalize ads. You can change this anytime in the Settings app. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles data.

Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for data storage, Apple for sign-in, Apple Music for music, the App Store for payments, and Google AdMob for ads.

Retention and Deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete all of your information on our servers (profile, posts, likes, friends, and more) with Delete Account in Settings. This also revokes the App's Sign in with Apple connection.

Sharing with Third Parties

We don't share personal information with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

Contact

For questions about this Policy, please use the contact information on the App's App Store page.
